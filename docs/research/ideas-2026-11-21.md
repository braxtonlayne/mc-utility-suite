# Ideas review, 2026-11-21

A read-only review of the current features, the menu screenshots and possible new modules. It is idea-level: check where each one plugs in before building it.

**Key finding:** the playtest screenshots are 1760x990 at GUI scale 4, so the screen is 440x247 GUI px. The menu takes 90% of that (396 px), which is under `UiTheme.THREE_COLUMN_MIN_W` (560). The compact fallback drops column 2 (search, legit preset, module list) and about three rows fit. The three-column layout has never appeared in a screenshot.

## A. Smarter upgrades to existing features

1. **Danger model and reflex scheduler (M-L).** `Autopilot.onTick()` is empty and `allows()` is only a permission gate.
   - Add an engine-free `DangerModel` in `core/autopilot`: a fixed array of hazards, each with time-to-impact and expected damage.
   - Feed it from `FallClutchPlanner`, `ThreatTracker`, `HazardScanner`, `CombatSnapshot`, health, air and fire.
   - Pick the reflex with the best damage averted per unit of time needed, counting turn time at 450°/s.
   - Evaluate only when a source has something to report.
2. **Start early instead of turning fast (S-M).** When fall risk rises (knockback near an edge, a fight on a ledge), only prepare: pull the clutch item into the hotbar and preselect the slot. The camera doesn't move until the player is airborne.
3. **Reflex chains on one curve (M).** Clutch, scoop, face the threat and escape as one continuous `RotationCurve` retarget under one EMERGENCY lease. Today the head returns to the run heading between reflexes.
4. **Threat escape and dodge (M).** Time a strafe tap to an arrow's arrival, and back off a creeper by its fuse ticks, using `ThreatTracker` data. It uses keys only, so it stays legit.
5. **Shield reflex (S).** Raise the shield against arrows, blasts and melee wind-ups; lower it to swing (`SwingGate`). Under Legit, only when the threat is already in the front cone.
6. **Clutch option ladder (M).** `FallClutchPlanner` would also rank:
   - deploying a worn elytra
   - an ender pearl to a ledge
   - air-steering into existing water

   Today the clutch does nothing while gliding.
7. **Humanization in the core (M).**
   - Turn duration by Fitts's law.
   - Log-normal reaction delay for non-emergency actions.
   - Aim points spread inside the hitbox.
   - A per-session skill seed.
   - A primitive RNG so nothing allocates.
8. **Tactic choice by utility plus an opponent model (M-L).**
   - Score fight, kite, retreat-heal and flee from DPS and health.
   - Keep ring buffers of opponent habits (strafe bias, crit-hop cadence, shield use).
   - The hunter bot can reuse this.
9. **Strip-route hazard cost (S-M).** Subtract fluid-pocket proximity in `StripRoutePlanner`. Add torch cadence and occasional wall glances.
10. **Black box and handover cue (S).**
    - Ring-buffer the last 10 s and dump it on death or big damage, with which reflex fired or declined and why.
    - Show a status line while a reflex acts ("Autopilot: clutch").
    - Release the camera with decaying momentum when manual input preempts.

## B. Menu polish (compact layout, as captured)

1. **Scale.** At GUI scale 3-4, render the menu at an effective integer scale of 2 and transform mouse coordinates. This brings back column 2 and roughly triples the visible rows.
2. **Goal placeholder.** It was truncated; switched to `firstThatFits`. Done 2026-11-21.
3. **Unlabeled Clean-Screen toggle next to Stop.** Add a "CLEAN" caption and rename Stop to "Stop all".
4. **Autopilot bar.** Replace the prose summary with four small reflex chips, lit or dim.
5. **Autopilot page.**
   - The sidebar still highlights Tools: clear the selection.
   - The card's own switch duplicates the bar: remove it.
   - Add a back control.
6. **Truncated hints and subtitles.** Wrap empty states to two lines and hide subtitles that don't fit. In Combat, remove the "Off" chip next to the switch.
7. **World presets.** Outline all four and fill only the applied one.
8. **Travel quick actions.**
   - Make all of them switches (Water Walk uses an "Off" chip plus a Start button).
   - "Take Me…" is cut off: label it "Go back".
9. **Tools.**
   - Collapse the six-line help paragraph to one line that expands.
   - Shorten "Pinned hotbar slots…" to "Pinned slots".
   - Paragraph grey `TEXT_MUTED` is hard to read: use `TEXT_DIM`.
