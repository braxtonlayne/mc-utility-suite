## Still open

- OreSim does not model the noise-based large ore veins (deep iron/copper with raw-ore blocks), so deepslate iron recall is low (about 30 %); stone iron and diamonds match well once chunks are simulated. Precision is limited by cave air the simulator cannot see.
- OreSim's per-chunk surface-height oracle (`generator.getBaseHeight` per column) allocates ~650 MB/s on the low-priority world thread while chunks are being simulated (it stops once the radius is done). A cheaper height source would help.
- The suite menu once closed right after Right Shift opened it (2 of 10 runs, not reproduced since). The harness now logs who closes the menu.
- The Combat tab has no toggle widgets, so the toggle round trip is not tested there.
- Whole-client tick time under the stress load is reported but not gated: it includes Baritone pathing for the running goal job and vanilla chunk loading. The suite's own module onTick total stays under 0.1 ms/tick.
